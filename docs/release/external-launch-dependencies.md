## Founding offer readiness

The 30-day founding campaign begins only at the recorded GA instant. Publish the future effective prices and exact end date; do not claim a former price. The 15-minute claim is a single-use server-side checkout-creation window that starts only after deliberate claim. The payment processor displays its real checkout expiry separately. Accountant and legal approval of pricing, tax display, refund, lifetime-service wording, and campaign copy is mandatory.

## Provider configuration record

For each provider store owner, account/team/project ID, environment, region, plan, approved spend, credential names (not values), webhook/callback URLs, data processed, retention, subprocessors/terms review date, test evidence, alert channel, rollback, and decommission procedure.
